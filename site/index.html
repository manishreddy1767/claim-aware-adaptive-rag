Why it is different

Private by design

The application runs on your own laptop. Your documents, questions and answers are stored on your disk and are never sent to a server. After installation it works offline.

Your GPU does the work

With an NVIDIA graphics card, answers take about a second. Without one, your processor does the work and answers are slower.

Checked, not guessed

Answers quote your files, or are written by a local AI model (qwen3:8b through Ollama) and then checked sentence by sentence. You are told when a question's assumption is wrong, when documents disagree, or when the answer is not there.

Install in four steps

  1. Download ClaimAwareRAG-Setup.exe and open it. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info → Run anyway.
  2. Follow the setup. Tick Install a local AI model if you want AI-written answers (about 5 GB more). Setup downloads what the app needs; this takes 10–30 minutes, once, and needs an internet connection.
  3. Open Claim-Aware RAG from the Start menu or your desktop. It opens in its own window.
  4. Create your account and add documents: upload files, add a whole folder from your laptop, or add a webpage. Then ask.

Questions

Where are my documents stored?

On your laptop, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ClaimAwareRAG. Files you add from a folder are read where they are and are not copied. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to install. Adding webpages also needs one, of course.

Can several people use it on one laptop?

Yes. Each person creates an account and sees only their own documents and questions.

How do I uninstall it?

Settings → Apps → Claim-Aware RAG → Uninstall. It asks whether to keep your documents. Ollama, if you installed it, is a separate app you can uninstall the same way.

How do AI-written answers work?

A model running on your laptop (qwen3:8b through Ollama) writes the answer from passages of your documents. Every sentence is then checked against your documents; sentences they do not support are removed, and you can see the model's original text. Turn it on in the app's AI settings. On a graphics card with less than 6 GB, an 8B model runs partly on the processor and answers take several seconds.
