Northstar Digital Employee Benefits Summary

Health Insurance
All full-time employees are covered by a group health insurance plan that includes their spouse and children.

Retirement Benefits
The company contributes to each employee's retirement fund as required by law.

Learning Allowance
Employees can claim a learning allowance of up to ₹20,000 per year for courses and certifications. Prior manager approval is required before enrolling in a course that will be reimbursed.

Wellness Benefit
Employees receive a wellness benefit of ₹5,000 per year for gym memberships, fitness classes or mental-health services.

Other Benefits
Employees should contact HR for clarification; no pet-adoption benefit is established by this document.
