Northstar Digital Work-From-Home Policy

Eligibility
Employees who have completed their probation period are eligible to work remotely. Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week, subject to eligibility and manager approval. Roles that require on-site equipment are not eligible for remote work.

Request Procedure
Work-from-home requests must be submitted through PeoplePortal by 3:00 p.m. on the preceding working day. Each request must specify the date, the work location and a brief reason. The reporting manager reviews and approves each request. Approval is not automatic.

Restrictions
Requests for more than 2 remote days per week require written approval from the department head and HR. Employees working remotely must remain reachable during core hours from 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
