Northstar Digital IT Equipment Policy

Laptops
Every new employee receives a company laptop on their first working day. Laptops are replaced every 3 years. Employees must return their laptop within 5 working days of leaving the company.

Security
Passwords must be changed every 90 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email and PeoplePortal. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours.

Personal Devices
Personal devices may be used for email only after the IT team installs the device-management app. Software that is not on the approved list must not be installed on company laptops.
