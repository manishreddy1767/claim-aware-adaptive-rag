Northstar Digital Attendance and Parental Leave

Attendance
Employees are expected to record their attendance in PeoplePortal every working day. Repeated late arrivals without notice may lead to a discussion with the reporting manager.

Maternity and Parental Leave
Eligible employees receive maternity leave according to applicable law and the company's parental-leave program. Parental leave for other parents is available under the same program. Employees should notify HR at least 8 weeks before the expected start of parental leave.
