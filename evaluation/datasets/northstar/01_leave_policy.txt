Northstar Digital Leave Policy

Annual Leave Entitlement
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid leave per calendar year. Employees joining during the year receive a prorated allowance based on their start date. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their contracted hours.

Requesting Leave
Leave requests must be submitted through PeoplePortal at least 5 working days in advance. Requests are approved by the employee's reporting manager. Leave of more than 10 consecutive days requires additional approval from HR.

Carry-Forward Rules
Up to 5 unused annual-leave days can be carried forward to the next calendar year. Carried-forward days must be used by March 31. Any carried-forward days not used by that date expire. Personal leave cannot be carried forward.
