Northstar Digital Travel FAQ (2026 update)

Allowances
The daily meal allowance for domestic travel is ₹3,000. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to ₹6,000 per night in metro cities.

Expense Claims
Travel expenses must be claimed within 45 days of returning from the trip.

Other Questions
Travel insurance for international trips is arranged by the TravelDesk team. Upgrades to business class are not covered by this FAQ.
