Northstar Digital Business Travel Policy

Booking
All business travel must be booked through the TravelDesk portal. Domestic trips must be booked at least 7 days before departure. International trips must be booked at least 21 days before departure and require approval from the department head.

Allowances
The daily meal allowance for domestic travel is ₹2,500. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to ₹6,000 per night in metro cities. Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours.

Expense Claims
Travel expenses must be claimed within 30 days of returning from the trip. Original receipts are required for every expense above ₹500. Claims submitted after the deadline are not reimbursed.
