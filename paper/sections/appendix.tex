\section{Local Generation with Verification and Revision}
\label{app:generation}
We let Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct \citep{qwen2024qwen25} draft answers from the retrieved evidence and pass the drafts through verification and revision, scoring against gold labels (72 answerable and 72 unanswerable SQuAD~2.0 test questions).
Without an answerability gate the model answered every unanswerable question; revision reduced the false-answer rate from 1.00 to 0.68 but lowered answer accuracy from 0.63 to 0.49, because the verifier also rejects correct paraphrases.
With the gate, revision reduced false answers from 0.25 to 0.17 (answer accuracy 0.57 to 0.44).
The extractive system was more accurate than any generative variant (overall accuracy 0.79 vs.\ at most 0.66).
These results come from \texttt{evaluation/run\_generation.py} with the zero-shot verifier.

\section{Implementation Details}
\label{app:implementation}
Retriever: $\lambda{=}0.65$, intent bonus $0.08$, initial $k{=}4$, maximum $k{=}10$, relative cutoff $0.7$, sufficiency score $0.45$ ($0.35$ with full key-term coverage), key-term coverage $0.6$, near-duplicate threshold $0.92$, two expansion rounds.
Verifier: six single-sentence candidates, windows around the top three, one multi-sentence premise (top-3 for the zero-shot model, top-5 for the fine-tuned model, matching its training premises), support and contradiction thresholds $0.7$, relevance threshold $0.40$ ($0.55$ for contradictions), conflict margin $0.10$.
Budget: $4$ calls per claim, minimum $6$ per answer, step size $2$, gap weight $0.7$, attempt penalty $0.8$, low-gain penalty $0.75$ (gain threshold $0.05$).
Fine-tuning: \texttt{cross-encoder/nli-deberta-v3-base}, 40{,}821 training examples (35\% of faithful claims kept), learning rate $2{\cdot}10^{-5}$, effective batch 16, two epochs, max length 320, frozen word embeddings, fp16; 1\,h on an RTX~3050. Held-out validation hallucination F1: 0.265 before, 0.605 after fine-tuning.
